# Credits and reused data

Every value in the database is cited to the paper, data sheet or mission record that states it;
those documents are listed with each simulant and each Moon site, and in every export.

## Reused data

**Lunar Regolith Database** — Gasteiner, L., Murdoch, N. & D'Angelo, O. (2026). *Mechanical
Properties of Lunar Soil: Raw Dataset for the Lunar Regolith Database.* Recherche Data Gouv,
version 2, last updated 27 August 2026. https://doi.org/10.57745/NTSZ8G

Licence: [Licence Ouverte / Open Licence 2.0 (etalab-2.0)](https://github.com/etalab/licence-ouverte/blob/master/LO.md).

Described in: Gasteiner, L., Murdoch, N. & D'Angelo, O. (2026). An Open Database of Lunar Regolith
and Simulants Properties. *International Journal for Numerical and Analytical Methods in
Geomechanics.* https://doi.org/10.1002/nag.70432

How it is used here:
- to find the original sources of simulant and Moon values; each value shown is cited to the
  original document, checked against it, and corrected where it differed;
- as the source of parts of the simulant descriptions (classification, application, feedstock,
  petrographic class) and of the list of Moon landing sites.

The authors and ISAE-SUPAERO do not endorse this database. The attribution is shown on the site
(Help → Sources and credits, and under each simulant's About), in every CSV export, and in
`public/data/data.json` (`credits`).
